Our Story

Crafted with love,
worn with joy

Hi, I'm Anusha Puvvada — the heart behind Anagha's Boutique. What started as a deep love for ethnic fashion has grown into a carefully curated collection of sarees, frocks, lehangas and custom blouses.

Every piece I offer is handpicked for its quality, colour and craftsmanship — whether it's a rich silk pattu saree for a wedding or a breezy organza frock for a family celebration. I love helping people find outfits that make them feel beautiful.

We offer worldwide shipping, so no matter where you are, Anagha's Boutique comes to you.
