Our Story

Dressed in tradition,
made with love

Hi, I'm Anusha Puvvada — the heart behind Anagha's Boutique. I started this boutique out of a genuine love for ethnic clothing and a desire to help women discover outfits that truly honour their roots.

From rich Mangalagiri and organza sarees to soft pastel long frocks, Kalamkari lehangas and finely embroidered custom blouses — every piece I curate is chosen with care. I work with each customer personally to make sure you find exactly what you're looking for, or create something made just for you.
